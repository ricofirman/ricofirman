## / about

I'm **Rico Firmansyah**, an Informatics student who enjoys turning
ideas into experiments—across robotics, artificial intelligence,
hardware, and the web.

I lead a **Search and Rescue (SAR) robotics team** and take part in
research with my academic supervisor. Alongside my studies, I'm
involved in organizational activities and an internship.

My interests span **computer vision with YOLO and ResNet,
AI agents, and AI integration with hardware**. I also build web
applications with **Laravel** and have recently started exploring
**drones and aerial robotics**.

> The most interesting part of an idea is finding out how it works in the real world.
